Dimensions and Formatting
Introduction
Dimensions are specifications. Dimensions in TDL are effective either in Display mode or in Print
mode. Data in TDL does not have an absolute position of the dim ensions specified, but relative .
There are four  definitions in TDL that attract dimensions. They are FORM, PA R T, LINE and
FIELD.
1. Unit of Measurement
A Unit of Measurement can be any of the following:
 Millimeters/mms
 Centimeters/cms
 Inch(es)
 Number of Characters/Number of Lines
 % Screen/Page
 Number – Points (where 1 Point = 1/72 Inch)
